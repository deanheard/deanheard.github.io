Pairly

Fair play, made simple.

Find Out More

Pairly is a fast, intelligent, and beautifully balanced doubles draw generator - built for clubs, coaches, and social players who want fair, repeatable matchups without the spreadsheets, arguments, or guesswork.

 

Inspired by the idea of a parley - a moment where people come together to agree on terms - Pairly helps players meet, mix, and match in a way that feels effortless and fair for everyone on court.

 

 

Why Pairly?

Pickleball sessions often start with the same question: “Who's playing who?”

Pairly answers that instantly. Just enter your players, choose your courts and rounds, and Pairly generates a complete doubles schedule that:

  • balances partners
  • avoids repeats
  • distributes court time fairly
  • adapts to any group size
  • and stays perfectly reproducible

Every draw is deterministic - meaning the same inputs always produce the same output. No surprises. No bias. No hidden randomness.

 

 

Fairness, engineered.

Pairly uses a custom scheduling engine designed specifically for pickleball doubles. It evaluates for each round:

  • games played
  • partner history
  • court usage
  • missed rounds
  • fairness targets
  • and even subtle rotation patterns
…to ensure every player gets a balanced experience.

It's not just random.

It's not just shuffled.

It's engineered fairness.

Shareable. Repeatable. Reliable.

Every draw is encoded into a compact URL you can:

  • share with your group
  • save for later
  • scan via QR code
  • reopen on any device
Even if your phone sleeps, reloads, or switches apps, Pairly restores your draw instantly. Perfect for club nights, coaching sessions, and tournaments.

 

 

Built for speed. Designed for clarity.

Pairly runs entirely in your browser - no accounts, no logins, no data stored on our servers. It's fast, lightweight, and works on any device.

 

 

A tool for organisers who care about fairness.

Whether you're running a weekly club night or organising a social ladder, Pairly gives you:

  • balanced matchups
  • clear schedules
  • reproducible draws
  • exportable CSVs
  • QR-shareable links
  • and a clean, modern interface
It's the simplest way to keep your players happy and your sessions running smoothly.
